Justin Allevato

Artist Identity & Profile

Justin Allevato is a modern independent musical artist cutting across cultural lines with native bilingual composition in Spanish and English. Fusing elements of contemporary Latin Pop, rhythmic Reggaeton, urban R&B, and infectious Afro-Latin vibes, his music captures sonic textures centered around romance, modern relationships, and deep emotional realities.
